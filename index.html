Home Care Services

Care designed around everyday life.

Our services help clients remain independent while giving families confidence, comfort, and peace of mind.

Personal Care

Bathing, hygiene support, grooming assistance, dressing support, and respectful daily care.

Companionship

Conversation, emotional support, social engagement, and a calm trusted presence at home.

Meal Preparation

Simple meals, hydration reminders, kitchen clean-up, and support with daily routines.

Transportation

Appointments, grocery shopping, pharmacy trips, and essential errands with dependable support.

Light Housekeeping

Laundry, tidying, organizing, and helping maintain a safe comfortable home environment.

Respite Care

Reliable relief for family caregivers who need time to rest, work, or care for themselves.

Where Care Feels Like Home

At Candle Grove, we believe exceptional care begins with genuine human connection. Our organization was founded on a simple promise: to provide dependable, compassionate support that helps individuals live with dignity, confidence, and peace of mind.

Every service we offer is guided by professionalism, personalized attention, and an unwavering commitment to enriching the lives of those we serve.

Candle Grove Home Care

Personalized support for everyday living.

Our flagship division provides thoughtful, non-medical home care tailored to the unique needs of each client and family. From companionship and personal care to meal preparation, transportation, and respite services, our caregivers create safe, comfortable, and meaningful experiences at home.
