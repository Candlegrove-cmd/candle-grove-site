Our Mission

Dependable care designed around everyday life.

Candle Grove Home Care provides dependable, compassionate, non-medical home services to help clients stay safely and comfortably in the place they know best — home.

We welcome conversations with families, hospitals, social workers, community partners, and referral sources seeking trusted support for individuals who need assistance at home.

Home Care Services

Support that feels personal, calm, and trusted.

Our services are designed to help clients remain independent while giving families confidence and peace of mind.

Personal Care

Bathing, hygiene support, grooming assistance, dressing support, and respectful daily care.

Companionship

Meaningful conversation, social engagement, emotional support, and calm presence at home.

Household Support

Light housekeeping, laundry, meal preparation, organization, and support with daily routines.

Errands & Transportation

Transportation to appointments, grocery shopping, pharmacy trips, and essential errands.

Medication Reminders

Gentle reminders to help clients follow their daily medication schedule as directed.

Respite Care

Reliable relief for family caregivers who need time to rest, work, or manage personal needs.
