About Candle Grove

Exceptional care begins with genuine human connection.

At Candle Grove Home Care, we believe exceptional care begins with genuine human connection. Our organization was founded on a simple promise: to provide dependable, compassionate support that helps individuals live with dignity, confidence, and peace of mind.

Every service we offer is guided by professionalism, personalized attention, and an unwavering commitment to enriching the lives of those we serve.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, compassionate, non-medical home services that help clients stay safely and comfortably in the place they know best—home.
