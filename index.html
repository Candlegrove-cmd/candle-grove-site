Personal Care

Companionship

Transportation

Ohio Medicaid & PASSPORT

Preparing for future Ohio Medicaid and PASSPORT waiver participation.

Candle Grove Home Care is currently focused on serving private-pay clients while preparing for future Ohio Medicaid and PASSPORT waiver provider participation as our agency grows.

We do not want to misrepresent our current status. At this time, we are not presenting ourselves as an active Medicaid or PASSPORT provider. We are building the agency foundation, policies, systems, and referral relationships needed for future participation.

Current Status

Private-pay clients are currently welcome. Medicaid and PASSPORT waiver participation is a future goal as Candle Grove Home Care continues to grow.
