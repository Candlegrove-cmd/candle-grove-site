Our Services

Care designed around everyday life.

Support at home for families who want dependable caregivers, flexible scheduling, and peace of mind.

Personal Care

Bathing, dressing, grooming, hygiene support, and respectful assistance with daily routines.

Companionship

Conversation, social engagement, emotional support, and a calm trusted presence at home.

Transportation

Appointments, errands, grocery shopping, pharmacy trips, and reliable community outings.

Meal Preparation

Simple meals, hydration reminders, kitchen clean-up, and support around nutrition routines.

Light Housekeeping

Laundry, tidying, organizing, and maintaining a safe and comfortable home environment.

Medication Reminders

Non-clinical reminders to help clients stay on schedule with prescribed routines.

Respite Care

Relief for family caregivers who need time to rest, work, travel, or care for themselves.

Errands & Shopping

Support with groceries, household needs, prescription pickup, and everyday tasks.

Aging-in-Place Support

Personalized care plans that help clients remain safe, comfortable, and independent at home.

Resources & Payment Support

Flexible care options for families.

Candle Grove currently focuses on Private Pay, Long-Term Care Insurance support, and veterans-related funding options when available.

We are preparing for future Ohio Medicaid and PASSPORT waiver provider participation as our agency grows.

Private Pay: Flexible scheduling for families who want dependable support designed around everyday life.
Long-Term Care Insurance: Guidance for families reviewing benefits, documentation, and care options.
