The Candle Grove Family of Services

One Trusted Name. Multiple Ways to Serve.

Candle Grove is building a connected family of services designed to support families, caregivers, employers, professionals, and the community through compassionate care, workforce solutions, education, consulting, and future outreach initiatives.

Candle Grove Home Care

Dependable, compassionate non-medical home care that helps individuals remain safe, comfortable, and independent at home.

  • Personal care assistance
  • Bathing and hygiene support
  • Companionship
  • Meal preparation
  • Medication reminders
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Transportation and errands
  • Respite care
  • Private Pay, LTC insurance, and veterans-related support

Candle Grove Workforce Solutions

Workforce and employment support designed to help employers, professionals, and caregivers complete hiring requirements with confidence.

  • Electronic fingerprinting
  • Ohio BCI background check support
  • FBI fingerprint check support
  • Employment screening assistance
  • Caregiver onboarding
  • Identity verification
  • Hiring documentation guidance
  • Compliance-focused workforce support

Candle Grove Education

Educational resources and training opportunities created to strengthen caregivers, support families, and improve care experiences.

  • Caregiver orientation
  • Dementia and Alzheimer's education
  • Communication training
  • Safety and infection prevention
  • Family caregiver workshops
  • Community wellness education
  • CPR and First Aid resource referrals
  • Continuing education opportunities

Candle Grove Consulting

Practical guidance for families and organizations navigating care decisions, planning, workforce needs, and community resources.

  • Home care planning
  • Family consultations
  • Long-term care planning discussions
  • Veterans benefit education
  • Long-term care insurance guidance
  • Community resource referrals
  • Caregiver recruitment consulting
  • Operational support for growing agencies

Candle Grove Foundation Future Vision

As Candle Grove grows, our future vision includes community outreach designed to support seniors, caregivers, veterans, and families through education, wellness initiatives, resource assistance, and meaningful partnerships.
