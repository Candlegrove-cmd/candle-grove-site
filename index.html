About Candle Grove

Exceptional care begins with genuine human connection.

At Candle Grove Home Care, we believe exceptional care begins with genuine human connection. Our organization was founded on a simple promise: to provide dependable, compassionate support that helps individuals live with dignity, confidence, and peace of mind.

Every service we offer is guided by professionalism, personalized attention, and an unwavering commitment to enriching the lives of those we serve.

Like the steady glow of a candle, we believe compassionate care brings comfort, guidance, and hope to every home we serve.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, compassionate, non-medical home services that help clients stay safely and comfortably in the place they know best—home.

Who We Serve

Support for every family journey.

Candle Grove proudly serves individuals and families from all backgrounds, cultures, and communities throughout Northeast Ohio.

Seniors Aging at Home

Support for individuals who want to remain safe, independent, and comfortable at home.

Family Caregivers

Respite and dependable assistance for families balancing care, work, and everyday life.

Veterans and Loved Ones

Guidance and support for eligible veterans and families exploring available home care resources.

Our Services

Care designed around everyday life.

Support at home for families who want dependable caregivers, flexible scheduling, and peace of mind.

Every care journey is unique. At Candle Grove Home Care, we provide personalized, non-medical support with compassion, professionalism, and respect—helping individuals remain safe, comfortable, and confident in the place they cherish most: home.

TransportationAppointments, errands, grocery shopping, pharmacy trips, and community outings.
Personal CareBathing, dressing, grooming, hygiene support, and respectful assistance.
CompanionshipMeaningful conversation, emotional support, and social engagement.
Meal PreparationSimple meals, hydration reminders, kitchen clean-up, and daily routines.
Light HousekeepingLaundry, tidying, organizing, and a safe comfortable home environment.
Medication RemindersNon-clinical reminders to help clients stay on schedule.
Respite CareRelief for family caregivers who need time to rest, work, or recharge.
Errands & ShoppingSupport with groceries, household needs, and prescription pickup.
Aging-in-Place SupportPersonalized care plans that support independence and safety at home.
