The Candle Grove Collection

Where Care Feels Like Home

At Candle Grove, we believe exceptional care begins with genuine human connection. Our organization was founded on a simple promise: to provide dependable, compassionate support that helps individuals live with dignity, confidence, and peace of mind.

Every service we offer is guided by professionalism, personalized attention, and an unwavering commitment to enriching the lives of those we serve.

Candle Grove Home Care

Personalized support for everyday living.

Our flagship division provides thoughtful, non-medical home care tailored to the unique needs of each client and family. From companionship and personal care to meal preparation, transportation, and respite services, our caregivers are dedicated to creating safe, comfortable, and meaningful experiences at home.

Personalized care plans Flexible scheduling Dependable, compassionate caregivers Support for aging in place Peace of mind for loved ones

Candle Grove Workforce Solutions

Supporting professionals. Strengthening organizations.

Workforce Solutions offers streamlined employment support and compliance services that simplify hiring and onboarding.

Electronic fingerprinting Ohio BCI and FBI background check support Employment screening assistance Caregiver onboarding Identity verification Compliance documentation guidance

Our goal is to help employers and professionals move forward with confidence while maintaining the highest standards of integrity and accountability.

Candle Grove Education

Learning that empowers compassionate care.

Education is at the heart of excellence. Candle Grove Education is being developed to provide practical resources, training opportunities, and professional development that strengthen caregivers, families, and communities.

Future programming may include caregiver orientation, dementia education, communication training, family workshops, safety education, and continuing learning opportunities.

Candle Grove Consulting

Trusted guidance for life's important decisions.

Families and organizations often face complex questions surrounding care planning, workforce development, and available resources. Candle Grove Consulting provides thoughtful guidance designed to simplify those decisions and support long-term success.

Areas of focus include care planning, family consultations, long-term care education, veterans-related information, long-term care insurance guidance, caregiver recruitment strategies, and operational support.
