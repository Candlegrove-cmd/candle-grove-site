About Candle Grove

Compassion, comfort, and dependable support for everyday living.

Candle Grove HomeCare is built on dignity, warmth, and trust. Our mission is to support seniors, adults with disabilities, individuals recovering at home, and families who need dependable daily living assistance for a loved one.


We provide non-medical care designed to make home life easier, safer, and more peaceful.

Our Services

Personalized non-medical support for clients and families.

Personal Care Assistance

Support with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, and daily routines.

Companionship

Meaningful conversation, emotional support, wellness check-ins, and help reducing loneliness.

Homemaker Services

Light housekeeping, laundry, meal preparation, organization, and maintaining a safe home.

Respite Care

Temporary relief for family caregivers who need time to rest, recharge, or manage responsibilities.

Errands & Transportation

Assistance getting to appointments, grocery shopping, errands, and essential outings.

Customized Care Plans

Flexible support based on each client’s schedule, needs, preferences, and family goals.
