About Candle Grove

Exceptional care begins with genuine human connection.

At Candle Grove Home Care, we believe exceptional care begins with genuine human connection. Our organization was founded on a simple promise: to provide dependable, compassionate support that helps individuals live with dignity, confidence, and peace of mind.

Every service we offer is guided by professionalism, personalized attention, and an unwavering commitment to enriching the lives of those we serve.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, compassionate, non-medical home services that help clients stay safely and comfortably in the place they know best—home.

Resources & Payment Support

Flexible care options for families.

Candle Grove currently focuses on Private Pay, Long-Term Care Insurance support, and veterans-related funding options when available. We are preparing for future Ohio Medicaid and PASSPORT waiver provider participation as our agency grows.

Private Pay: Flexible scheduling for families who want dependable support designed around everyday life.
Long-Term Care Insurance: Guidance for families reviewing benefits, documentation, and care options.
Veterans Support: Information for eligible veterans and families exploring available care resources.
